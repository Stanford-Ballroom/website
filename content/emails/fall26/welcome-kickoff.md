Hi everyone, and welcome to Stanford Ballroom!

It was lovely meeting so many of you at GOALIE and TREEFEST! If you signed up at either activities fair, you're now on our mailing list :). We're so excited to welcome you, whether you've danced before or have never taken a dance class!

And to everyone already on the list: **sorry for the many emails this week!** We've had lots of updates as the quarter gets going, and wanted to make sure our newest members have the details too.

Website: [ballroom.stanford.edu](https://ballroom.stanford.edu)

{{< email-event title="Monday, October 5 — Fall Kickoff!" when="From 7:30 pm" where="Roble 113, then Roble 115 (375 Santa Teresa St.)" >}}
Come join our **fall kickoff this Monday, October 5**! We're partnering with **Dancebreak**, who will be showcasing Stanford's social dancing groups starting at **7:30 pm in Roble 113**.

Our performance is scheduled for **around 7:45 pm**, and then we'll head over to **Roble 115 for newcomer lessons**, followed by **pizza and snacks**!

**Please invite your friends!** Come try some dancing, meet the team, and get to know your fellow newcomers :).
{{< /email-event >}}

{{< email-notice title="New to Ballroom?" >}}
**Beginners are always welcome! No dance experience is needed, and no registration is required. You also don't require a partner!** We frequently rotate partners in class, so just bring yourself :). We're open to all Stanford affiliates.

**You don't have to audition or compete to join us;** come learn, meet people, and enjoy dancing! Wear comfortable clothes that let you move freely, and bring socks or non-marking dance shoes/sneakers.
{{< /email-notice >}}

{{< email-event title="Classes Every Monday" when="7:30 – 9:30 pm" where="Usually Roble 115 (375 Santa Teresa St.)" >}}
Beyond kickoff, we have classes **every Monday evening from 7:30 to 9:30 pm**, usually in **Roble 115**. We offer lessons for both complete beginners and more experienced dancers, with professional coaches :).

Keep an eye on this mailing list and our [schedule](/schedule/) for weekly details and any room or time changes. We'd love to see you throughout the quarter!
{{< /email-event >}}
