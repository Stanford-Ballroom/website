## Leadership Election for Next Year!

It's election time! Are you enjoying your time at Stanford Ballroom? Perhaps you'd like to be involved in shaping the club's vision or giving back by helping it run smoothly. This is your chance to be on the other side and be part of the decision making :). Personally, being part of leadership has been an extremely precious experience. I have really enjoyed helping shape the club and seeing all of you grow. I hope you join us! See more details, including a link to the nomination form, below.

{{< email-button href="https://forms.gle/Y5nrYxfJZKZQZWaGA" label="Nomination form — due June 1 before class" >}}

Please self-nominate yourself for any of the positions listed. Deadline: next Monday, June 1, before our final class. Once nominations are collected, we'll have a one week voting period, and confirm final leadership on June 8. You must be a current undergraduate or graduate student at Stanford to vote and be eligible for office.

I will be standing up again for President but will have to be elected again. So, I encourage you to stand up if you're interested!

I would like to highlight two crucial roles looking for new members to fill in: (1) **Financial Officer (Treasurer)**, and (2) **Director of Communications (Social Media)**. Please join me in thanking Dasha for a phenomenal tenure, first as President, and later, as Financial Officer. The club has progressed so smoothly due to her relentless dedication toward making things work and making ballroom accessible to you all (for little to no cost to you)! It's now time for her to rest and focus on what's next, but she will of course be around to facilitate the transition.

Please reach out to me, Dasha, or the relevant officer to learn more about the roles. Feel free to email, text, or find us in-person.

Nominees may choose to prepare a short proposal to share with everyone. This is optional. Please share it in the comments section of the form or have it ready by Jun 1. This can be a great way to share your vision or discuss topics of interest.
